Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Suspension Tuner is made by Eddie Sisomsun. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Suspension Tuner app on iPhone, iPad or Mac.

The short version

Suspension Tuner does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information you enter

The weight, height, frame size, riding style and suspension model you enter are used only to calculate your setup. The calculation happens entirely on your device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Sharing, printing and saving

If you choose to share your setup, print it or save it as an image, the app uses your device's built-in share sheet and printing. Where it goes is up to you. If you save an image to Photos, the app asks for permission to add it, and it only adds the image you chose to save.

Links to other websites

Tapping a model name opens that manufacturer's website in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies, which we don't control.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
