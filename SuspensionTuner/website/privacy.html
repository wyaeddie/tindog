Suspension Tuner for iPhone and iPad

Privacy policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Suspension Tuner is made by Whole Stack Solutions. This policy explains what happens to the information you use in the app.

Data not collected
  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No ads or third-party SDKs
  • Everything stays on your device

Your bikes and setups

To calculate a setup, the app uses what you enter: your weight and height, frame size, riding style and riding conditions, your fork and shock models and travel, bike details such as wheel size, frame type and colour, hours since service, and the settings and notes you save to History.

All of it is calculated and stored on your device only. We never receive it and the app does not send it anywhere.

  • The app keeps your bikes in a storage area shared with its own home-screen widget, so the widget can show your pump numbers. That storage stays on your device.
  • Your device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup, if you use them) may include the app's data, as with any app. Those backups are managed by Apple under your settings.

Suspension Tuner Pro

Suspension Tuner Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, email or payment details. The app only asks the App Store on your device whether Pro has been purchased, so it can unlock Pro features and restore them on your other devices. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing, printing and Photos

If you choose to share a setup, print it or save it as an image, the app uses your device's built-in share sheet and printing. Where it goes is up to you. If you save an image to Photos, iOS asks for permission to add it, and the app only adds the image you chose to save. The app never reads your photo library.

Links to other websites

Tapping a model name or a source badge opens the maker's website or setup guide in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies, which we don't control.

Deleting your data

To remove everything, go to Settings → Erase all bikes and history in the app, or delete the app from your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing else to delete. A Pro purchase stays with your Apple ID and can be restored at any time.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you the right to access, correct or delete personal data a company holds about you. Suspension Tuner doesn't collect or hold personal data, so there is nothing for us to provide or delete, but you're welcome to contact us with any question.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version of the app ever collects data, this policy will say so before that version is released.
