Suspension Tuner website
========================

Upload everything in this folder (index.html, launch.html, privacy.html, styles.css and the assets folder)
to your web host, keeping the same folder layout.

Before uploading, open index.html in a text editor and replace:
  - href="#download" on the Download / App Store buttons -> your App Store link

Support email (already set): eddie@wholestack.solutions

Links for App Store Connect:
  - Marketing URL:       https://yoursite.com/            (index.html)
  - Support URL:         https://yoursite.com/#faq
  - Privacy Policy URL:  https://yoursite.com/privacy.html

launch.html is the one-page launch site (release November 2, 2026). It is self-contained: the
screenshots are built in, so it only needs privacy.html beside it. After launch, point its
"Coming to the App Store" buttons (href="#download") at the App Store link; the countdown switches
to "Out now" by itself on November 2.
